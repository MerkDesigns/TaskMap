## About

TaskMap is a dark-mode desktop canvas for visually organizing projects, tasks, notes, images, and ideas. Arrange everything freely across multiple workspaces while keeping your data stored locally on your computer.

## Features

- **Flexible canvases** — Pan, zoom, and organize work across multiple independent canvases.
- **Containers and cards** — Group related tasks and reorder cards with drag and drop.
- **Rich content** — Add text cards, Markdown text blocks, images, colors, and hyperlinks.
- **Element extensions** — Add functionality such as checkboxes, counters, privacy, locking, search, and sorting only where you need it.
- **Fast navigation** — Use canvas previews, a minimap, snapping, multi-selection, and layer controls.
- **Safe editing** — Undo and redo changes, copy and paste elements, and import or export your data.
- **Local persistence** — Your work is saved locally and remains available between sessions.
- **Automatic updates** — Receive new TaskMap releases through the built-in updater.
